=== EXPERIMENTS ===
All experiments are conducted using machines equipped with NVIDIA A5000 GPUs, with PyTorch 2.0.1 and CUDA 11.7 as the software environment. For comparison methods that exceed the memory capacity of the A5000 setup, we employ NVIDIA A800 GPUs to ensure reliable execution and fair evaluation. Unless otherwise specified, we adopt the same hyperparameter settings as 3DGS . For outdoor scenes in the TNT dataset , we incorporate decoupled appearance modeling to mitigate exposure-related artifacts. All models are trained and evaluated on the same data splits as used in 2DGS , across the TNT and Mip-NeRF360 benchmarks.

=== CONCLUSION ===
Although our method can achieve high-resolution scene reconstruction under limited GPU resources, extending the block-based framework to dynamic scenes introduces new challenges. In particular, ensuring temporal consistency across frames and accurately modeling motion across spatial partitions remain open problems. Addressing these challenges will be a key focus of our future work, with the goal of enabling temporally coherent and spatially consistent reconstruction in dynamic environments.
