\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conc}

We have presented a novel framework for sparse-view novel view synthesis that effectively addresses the geometry-appearance discrepancy problem in 3D Gaussian Splatting. 
Through robust geometric regularization, geometry-guided appearance optimization, our method achieves joint optimization of geometry and appearance without relying on external depth priors. Extensive experiments demonstrate state-of-the-art performance with reduced floater artifacts and improved geometric accuracy.

% While our photometric consistency framework shows promising results, it may struggle with severe illumination changes or highly specular surfaces. Future work could explore incorporating learned appearance models to handle such challenging scenarios. Nevertheless, our work establishes an effective paradigm for high-quality sparse-view novel view synthesis.
