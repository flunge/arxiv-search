\begin{abstract}
Streaming reconstruction from uncalibrated monocular video remains challenging, as it requires both high-precision pose estimation and computationally efficient online refinement in dynamic environments. While coupling 3D foundation models with SLAM frameworks is a promising paradigm, a critical bottleneck persists: most multi-view foundation models estimate poses in a feed-forward manner, yielding pixel-level correspondences that lack the requisite precision for rigorous geometric optimization. To address this, we present \textbf{M}$^3$, which augments the \textbf{M}ulti-view foundation model with a dedicated \textbf{M}atching head to facilitate fine-grained dense correspondences and integrates it into a robust \textbf{M}onocular Gaussian Splatting SLAM. M$^3$ further enhances tracking stability by incorporating dynamic area suppression and cross-inference intrinsic alignment. Extensive experiments on diverse indoor and outdoor benchmarks demonstrate state-of-the-art accuracy in both pose estimation and scene reconstruction. Notably, M$^3$ reduces ATE RMSE by 64.3\% compared to VGGT-SLAM 2.0 and outperforms ARTDECO by 2.11 dB in PSNR on the ScanNet++ dataset.
    % Streaming(on-the-fly) 3D reconstruction from uncalibrated monocular video remains a significant challenge, particularly in unconstrained, dynamic environments. While integrating 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) with deep visual SLAM and foundation models has advanced the field, existing frameworks struggle to fully exploit multi-view consistency. They typically rely on decoupled tracking and mapping paradigms or lack the explicit pixel-level correspondences needed for robust classical optimization. Furthermore, these frameworks are highly vulnerable to dynamic objects, which corrupt pose estimation and cause catastrophic trajectory drift. In this work, we propose M$^3$, an efficient and robust streaming reconstruction framework. We augment a multi-view 3D foundation model with a dense matching head, enabling a tightly coupled architecture that unifies frontend tracking and backend global bundle adjustment to natively enforce multi-view consistency. To handle real-world complexities, we introduce a descriptor-based motion estimation module that explicitly filters transient regions during optimization and 3DGS initialization. Finally, we formulate camera tracking and global optimization within the $Sim(3)$ manifold, integrated with a hierarchical loop closure strategy, to rigorously eliminate scale drift. Extensive experiments demonstrate that M$^3$ achieves highly accurate tracking and globally consistent, high-fidelity reconstructions across diverse scenes.

\end{abstract}
