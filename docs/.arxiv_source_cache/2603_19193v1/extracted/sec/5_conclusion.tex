\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
In this work, we present Splat2BEV, a novel Gaussian Splatting–assisted framework for BEV perception. Unlike existing implicit end-to-end BEV prediction methods,
Splat2BEV incorporates explicit 3D scene reconstruction into the BEV perception pipeline, enabling the learning of geometry-aligned BEV representations.
Comprehensive experiments across multiple downstream tasks demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach and validate the high quality of the learned geometry-aligned features.
