\section*{Impact Statement}
This paper presents work whose goal is to achieve photorealistic and real-time novel view synthesis for dynamic scenes.  
Therefore, we acknowledge that our approach can potentially be used to generate fake images or videos.
We firmly oppose the use of our research for disseminating false information or damaging reputations.
