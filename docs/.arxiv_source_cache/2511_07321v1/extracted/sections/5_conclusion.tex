\section{Conclusion}
\vspace{-4mm}
In this work, we introduced \ourmethod, a versatile feedforward model for high-quality 3D Gaussian reconstruction from an arbitrary number of images, uniquely capable of operating in both pose-free/pose-dependent and calibrated/uncalibrated settings. We address two key challenges: the entanglement of geometry and pose learning, and scale ambiguity. Our novel \emph{mix-forcing} training strategy resolves the former by balancing training stability and mitigating exposure bias. For the latter, we combine a robust \emph{max pairwise distance normalization} with an \emph{Intrinsic Condition Embedding (ICE)} module that enables reconstruction from uncalibrated inputs. These contributions significantly advance the flexibility and robustness of feedforward 3D reconstruction.
