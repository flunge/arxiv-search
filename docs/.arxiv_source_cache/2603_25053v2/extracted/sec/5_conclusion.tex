\section{Conclusion}
We present \ours, a geometry-informed video generative model that enhances 3DGS reconstructions by leveraging depth, normals, and uncertainty cues.
It effectively removes floaters, flickering, and blur while preserving fine details.
Trained with a diverse artifact synthesis pipeline, \ours achieves state-of-the-art performance, and its distilled few-step variant enables real-time inference.
