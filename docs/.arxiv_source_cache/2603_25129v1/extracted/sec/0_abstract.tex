\begin{abstract}
  While 3D Vision Foundation Models (3DVFMs) have demonstrated remarkable zero-shot capabilities in visual geometry estimation, their direct application to generalizable novel view synthesis (NVS) remains challenging.
  In this paper, we propose \textbf{AirSplat}, a novel training framework that effectively adapts the robust geometric priors of 3DVFMs into high-fidelity, pose-free NVS. Our approach introduces two key technical contributions: 
  (1) \textit{Self-Consistent Pose Alignment} (SCPA), a training-time feedback loop that ensures pixel-aligned supervision to resolve pose-geometry discrepancy; and 
  (2) \textit{Rating-based Opacity Matching} (ROM), which leverages the local 3D geometry consistency knowledge from a sparse-view NVS teacher model to filter out degraded primitives.
  Experimental results on large-scale benchmarks demonstrate that our method \textit{significantly} outperforms state-of-the-art pose-free NVS approaches in reconstruction quality.
  Our AirSplat highlights the potential of adapting 3DVFMs to enable simultaneous visual geometry estimation and high-quality view synthesis.
  \keywords{3D Reconstruction \and 3D Vision \and Gaussian Splatting}
\end{abstract}
