\section{Conclusion}
\label{section: conclusion}
We propose \titleDef, a driving world model that enhances instance-level temporal consistency and spatial geometric fidelity. Our approach introduces two key advancements: the Instance Flow Guider module, which extracts and propagates instance features across frames to preserve instance identity over time, and the Spatial Geometric Aligner module, which ensures precise instance positioning and explicitly models occlusion hierarchies. By incorporating these instance-aware mechanisms, \titleDef~achieves SOTA generation quality and significantly improves downstream autonomous driving tasks. Finally, we leverage CARLA’s autopilot to generate rare yet safety-critical driving scenarios, demonstrating the immense potential of \titleDef~ in long-tail simulation.
