Abstract
Transformers have emerged as a universal backbone across
3D perception, video generation, and world models for au-
tonomous driving and embodied AI, where understanding
camera geometry is essential for grounding visual observa-
tions in three-dimensional space. However, existing camera
encoding methods often rely on simplified pinhole assump-
